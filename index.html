DNS leak auditing, Encrypted Client Hello and a network watch for Linux

Eighteen field-tested command-line tools, each written after a real outage that took hours to diagnose by hand. They audit everything that can set your DNS and show where a leak could get in. Fix a specific problem

Wi-Fi connected, but no internetDiagnose it layer by layer: link, routes, DNS, a leftover VPN or kill-switch, a captive portal. DNS leak test on LinuxWhich resolver is really answering under systemd-resolved, and whether DNSSEC and DoT actually work. Encrypted Client Hello on LinuxWhat ECH hides, a curl that can speak it, and switching it on in Firefox and Chromium browsers.

Why this exists

Most network trouble on a modern Linux desktop isn't a routing problem. It's a DNS problem that looks like one. Your VPN pushed its own resolver, DHCP quietly overrode the one you configured, systemd-resolved is answering from a per-link server you never chose, or a kill-switch left an nftables table behind after its daemon died.

netmaster exists because a Wi-Fi link associated fine, the LAN worked, and the internet was gone, and stayed gone across reboots. The cause was a stale VPN tun device that NetworkManager had adopted and persisted, with hundreds of static routes. probesource exists because "who is this address and why is it knocking" deserved a better answer than four terminals of whois, dig and tcpdump.

Quick start

git clone https://github.com/doug445/Panoptes.git
cd Panoptes
sudo ./install.sh
checkdns              # resolver, WARP, mDNS and ECH status in one readout
sudo audit-dns.sh     # what controls DNS here, and where a leak could get in
netcheck              # interfaces, Wi-Fi band/bitrate, firewall, whois
ech-browsers.sh       # turn on Encrypted Client Hello in every browser you have

Nothing installs a service or changes your system until you ask it to. audit-dns.sh and netcheck are strictly read-only. The installer works out which of the suite's ~45 requirements are missing and installs them with dnf, apt, pacman, zypper or apk.

FAQ

Which Linux distributions does Panoptes support?

It is built and used daily on Fedora, and tested on Linux Mint, Manjaro, EndeavourOS, Debian/Ubuntu and Fedora Asahi Remix. Anything running systemd-resolved and NetworkManager is in scope, and its dependency installer understands dnf, apt, pacman, zypper and apk.

Will installing it change my system?

Nothing installs a service or changes your system until you ask it to. audit-dns.sh and netcheck are strictly read-only, harden-dns.sh supports --dry-run, and wifi-recover.sh diagnoses without changing anything unless you pass --repair.

Why does turning Cloudflare WARP off make my browser think it is offline?

DNS lookups in flight at that moment fail DNSSEC validation because their transport disappeared mid-chain, and systemd-resolved caches the failures. resolvectl flush-caches clears them. FallbackDNS= does not help: it is only used when DNS= is empty.

Does it touch Tor Browser or Mullvad Browser?

No. ech-browsers.sh never touches them: they resolve through their proxy circuit on purpose, and pointing them at a DoH resolver would send lookups around it.

On a Mac? The macOS counterpart is macOS-SecureNetwork-Suite.
